25 Years of Industry Experience

Gee Tech Service is a multi-disciplinary technical service provider specializing in Mechanical, Electrical, and Instrumentation (MEI) solutions. With 25 years of industry experience, we support businesses across their entire project lifecycle — from installation to maintenance to breakdown support — so their operations run safely, efficiently, and without unplanned downtime.

Our experience spans the practical execution of industrial technical work — from installation and wiring to calibration, fabrication, erection, commissioning, maintenance, and breakdown support.
